II.

I am a documentary producer and multimedia journalist with more than a decade of experience making complex subjects clear and resonant for international audiences. From 2017 to 2025 I worked for the BBC World Service, producing video explainers, longform features, and documentaries — including coverage of the Russia–Ukraine war — across Moscow, London, and Riga.

My documentary work has focused on human rights and social issues in under-covered parts of Russia. Films I have directed have been nominated for the independent Redkollegia prize, broadcast on BBC UK, and recognized at international festivals including FilmPride and the Harvey Milk Festival. One triggered an official investigation that led to a criminal conviction.

Before journalism, I produced cultural and public programmes — the Buta Festival of Azerbaijani Arts in London, Moscow's Night at the Museum (1.2M visitors), and the first Russian shows by Scissor Sisters and The White Stripes. I mentor editorial teams at independent media outlets and lecture on storytelling and narrative structure.
